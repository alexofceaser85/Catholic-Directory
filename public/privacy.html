Privacy

What this directory collects, who sees it, and how to have it removed. Last updated October 17, 2026.

What you give us when you post

  • Public details: the title, category, description, contact email, and anything optional you add (phone, address, website, links, photos). Once an admin approves the post, anyone visiting the directory can see these.
  • Your name: your first and last name. Only our admins see it, to review the post. It is never published.

Photos are re-saved when uploaded, which removes hidden information such as the location where they were taken.

What we use it for

Only to run the directory: showing approved posts, reviewing new posts and edits, and emailing you about your own post (your private "manage your post" link, and occasional check-ins asking whether a listing is still current). We don't sell or share your information, show ads, or send newsletters.

Reports

If someone reports a post as out of date, we store their reason so an admin can review it. Reports don't ask for a name or email.

Cookies and tracking

No analytics, ads or tracking. The only cookie is a sign-in cookie for our admins. Visitors and posters get none. Like most websites, our hosting provider keeps short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses) to keep the site running and stop abuse.

Google services

Admins sign in with Google, which tells us only their name and email address, to check that they're on the admin list. The directory sends its emails from its own Gmail account through Google's Gmail service. It uses only permission to send mail from that account, never reads it, and never accesses a visitor's Google account. Information received from Google is used only for these purposes and is not shared or sold.

Where it's stored

Posts and photos are stored in a database at our hosting provider (Railway, in the United States). Emails pass through Google's Gmail service.

Changing or removing your post

Use the link in your email, or Manage your post to have a new link sent, to edit or remove your post at any time. A removed post disappears from the directory right away. To have everything about your post deleted for good, including your name, email us and we'll do it.
